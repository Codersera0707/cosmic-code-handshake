# Cosmic Code Handshake

I need an animation effect to insert into the intro of my portfolio site.

In other words, I must reveal my identity as a developer through code, not video.

Mathematical calculations and a fantastical, cosmic atmosphere are important.

I can think of something like a CANVAS as a stack.

What I envision is that on the left, multiple rays of light twist to form "business," while on the right, a few rays of light and intelligent brain matter complete the design.

These two, accompanied by a strong light, slowly come together to form a handshake—that is, two hands clasped tightly.

As they slowly fade away, much like water draining from a bottle neck, they form a diamond, and as it bursts, the intro ends.
